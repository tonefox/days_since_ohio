# Constitution — Preamble

> **Constitutional layer.** This document is part of the durable core of the kit. It changes
> rarely and only through the amendment process (`constitution/03-amendment-process.md`). Adapt
> tone and examples to your organization via `brand.yaml`; do not alter the principles casually.

## Why this exists

Partner enablement was built for a slower world. Its instruments — fixed certifications,
versioned courseware, scheduled training events, instructor-led bootcamps — assume that what a
partner needs to know is stable enough to package once and deliver many times. That assumption
no longer holds.

Three forces have broken it:

1. **The half-life of knowledge is collapsing.** AI-era products change on weekly and monthly
   cadences. Enablement content authored against a specific release is often stale before it is
   delivered. The traditional response — rebuild the curriculum each cycle — does not scale.

2. **Enablement cost scales linearly with reach.** To enable more partners, organizations have
   historically needed proportionally more trainers, more content, more events, and more
   coordination. Reach and cost rise together. In a large partner ecosystem this becomes the
   binding constraint.

3. **Enablement lags the technology it teaches.** By the time a certification path is designed,
   reviewed, and published, the capability it certifies has moved. Partners are perpetually
   trained for the product of six months ago.

AI is simultaneously the accelerant of these problems and the means to resolve them. The same
technology that shortens the half-life of knowledge can author, personalize, deliver, and assess
enablement at a marginal cost approaching zero — **if** the enablement system is designed to
exploit it. That design is the purpose of this kit.

## The thesis: asymmetric scaling

A correctly designed enablement system should exhibit **asymmetric (super-linear) scaling**:

- It is **technology-agnostic at the level of principle.** What a partner must be able to *do* —
  qualify, position, design, integrate, and prove value — is durable. The specific product,
  version, or feature through which they do it is not. Principles are written against outcomes,
  not features, so they survive technology change.

- It delivers **disproportionate returns when AI is applied to the enablement function itself.**
  As an organization moves AI from authoring, to delivery, to assessment, to self-updating
  content, the marginal cost of enabling each additional partner trends toward zero while the
  *currency* and *quality* of enablement rise. Reach and cost decouple. That decoupling is the
  asymmetry.

An enablement program that merely *teaches people about AI* captures none of this. A program
that is *itself operated through AI*, governed by durable principles, captures all of it.

## Why "constitutional"

This framework is not a course, a curriculum, or a one-size-fits-all program. It is a
**constitution**: a small set of enduring principles, plus a governance mechanism that defines
how those principles are interpreted, instantiated, enforced, and amended for each organization
that adopts them.

This separation is deliberate and load-bearing:

| Layer | Constitutional analogy | Rate of change | Operated by |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Principles** | The Constitution | Rarely | Human leadership |
| **Instantiation** | Statutes | Continuously | AI-generated, organization-specific |
| **Enforcement** | The Judiciary | Continuously | AI-assessed competency |
| **Amendment** | Ratification | As the ecosystem learns | Outcome-driven feedback |

The durable layer gives the framework portability — it can be carried into any organization and
any technology. The instantiation layer gives it specificity — every adopting organization
expresses the principles in its own language, brand, vertical, and product set. The enforcement
layer gives it teeth. The amendment layer keeps it current.

The result is a standard, not a script. It is defined centrally and enforced consistently, yet
interpreted locally — what this kit calls **federated sovereignty**. An accelerating partner
(the organization that introduces this framework downstream) maintains the constitutional core;
each adopting organization forks it, customizes it, and operates it, while improvements flow back
upstream.

## How to read this kit

- **`constitution/`** — the durable core: the Articles, the maturity model, and the amendment
  process. Read this first; it is the part you must internalize to speak to the framework.
- **`assessment/`** — the diagnostic that locates an organization on the maturity model and
  routes it to the appropriate delivery path.
- **`competency-maps/`** — the same principles expressed as demonstrable outcomes for sales and
  technical roles.
- **`playbook/`** — how to run an engagement end to end, including the two delivery paths and an
  executive summary for leadership buy-in.
- **`templates/`** — reusable skeletons for building assessments and facilitation sessions.
- **`brand.yaml` + `customize.py`** — the mechanism that turns this shared core into your own
  branded, organization-specific kit.

The remainder of the constitution begins with the Articles.
