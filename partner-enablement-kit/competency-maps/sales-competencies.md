# Sales Competency Map

> **Instantiation layer.** This map expresses the constitution's principles as demonstrable
> outcomes for partner **sales** roles. The competencies are durable; the examples, product
> references, and proficiency evidence should be adapted to your organization via `brand.yaml`.

Every competency below is written as a **demonstrable outcome** (Article I) against customer value
rather than product features (Article II). Certification of each competency requires demonstrated
performance, not recall (Article V). "AI fluency in the role" is a standing competency, not an
elective (Article VI).

## How to read this map

- **Competency** — what the seller must be able to do.
- **Demonstration** — how it is proven, under realistic conditions.
- **Outcome signal** — the result it should move (Article VIII).

Proficiency is expressed in three tiers — **Foundational → Practitioner → Advisor** — defined at
the end. A partner's path (Article IV) is assembled from the competencies they have not yet
demonstrated at the required tier.

## Domain 1 — Customer and Value

### S1. Qualify a fit-for-purpose opportunity
- **Demonstration:** given a realistic account scenario, qualify in or out against fit criteria
  and articulate the business reason, distinguishing a fit-for-purpose deal from a forced one.
- **Outcome signal:** quality of qualified pipeline; win rate on qualified deals.

### S2. Articulate value in the customer's terms
- **Demonstration:** translate capabilities into the customer's business outcomes and quantify
  the value with a defensible business case, without relying on feature lists.
- **Outcome signal:** progression rate from qualified to committed.

### S3. Run outcome-led discovery
- **Demonstration:** conduct a discovery conversation that surfaces the customer's desired
  outcomes, constraints, and decision process, and adapt positioning accordingly.
- **Outcome signal:** stage conversion; reduction in stalled deals.

## Domain 2 — Positioning and Competition

### S4. Position against alternatives
- **Demonstration:** position the solution against named alternatives and the "do nothing"
  option, on the customer's evaluation criteria rather than on feature parity.
- **Outcome signal:** competitive win rate.

### S5. Build and defend the business case
- **Demonstration:** construct an ROI/total-value narrative and defend it under challenge from a
  skeptical economic buyer.
- **Outcome signal:** average deal value; discount discipline.

## Domain 3 — AI Fluency in the Sales Role (Article VI)

### S6. Use AI for research and account preparation
- **Demonstration:** use AI tools to assemble account intelligence, identify stakeholders, and
  prepare a call plan — and critically evaluate the output for accuracy and bias before acting on
  it.
- **Outcome signal:** seller productivity; preparation quality.

### S7. Use AI in discovery and value articulation
- **Demonstration:** use AI to draft discovery questions, value hypotheses, and tailored
  business-case language, then exercise judgment to adapt them to the live customer.
- **Outcome signal:** cycle time; progression rate.

### S8. Apply AI responsibly and within guardrails
- **Demonstration:** identify what may and may not be shared with AI tools, recognize
  hallucination and over-reliance risks, and follow the organization's responsible-use
  guardrails.
- **Outcome signal:** governance/compliance exceptions (trending down).

## Domain 4 — Deal Execution

### S9. Orchestrate a multi-stakeholder deal
- **Demonstration:** map a buying committee, sequence stakeholder engagement, and navigate a
  realistic procurement and approval process to close.
- **Outcome signal:** slip rate; time-to-close on committed deals.

## Proficiency tiers

| Tier | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| **Foundational** | Can perform the competency in a structured scenario with guidance; judgment is developing. |
| **Practitioner** | Performs the competency independently in realistic conditions; sound judgment under normal complexity. |
| **Advisor** | Performs under high complexity and ambiguity, and can coach others — the train-the-trainer tier for Path A. |

## Using this map

- **Assessment:** each demonstration maps to a scenario or artifact built from
  `templates/assessment-builder-template.md`.
- **Pathing:** a seller's adaptive path (Article IV) is the set of competencies not yet
  demonstrated at the required tier for their role.
- **Train-the-trainer (Path A):** the **Advisor** tier defines who is qualified to cascade
  enablement to the wider field.
- **Accountability:** programs report against the **outcome signals** named above, not against
  completion.
