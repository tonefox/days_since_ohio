# Brand assets

Place your organization's brand assets here and reference them from `brand.yaml`.

- **`logo_path` in `brand.yaml`** defaults to `assets/logo.png`. Drop your logo at that path
  (or update `logo_path` to point at your file). `customize.py` copies this folder into the
  branded `dist/` output so the result is self-contained.

This folder is part of your **local customization** layer (see `CONTRIBUTING.md`) — its contents
are yours and are not part of the constitutional core.
