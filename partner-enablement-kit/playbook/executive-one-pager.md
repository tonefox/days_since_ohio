# Constitutional Enablement — Executive Summary

> **Instantiation layer.** A one-page summary for leadership buy-in (the **Ratify** stage of the
> engagement lifecycle). Brand and tailor via `brand.yaml`.

## The problem

Partner enablement was built for a slower world. Fixed certifications and courseware assume a
partner's required knowledge is stable enough to package once and deliver many times. In the
AI era that assumption fails on three fronts: the **half-life of knowledge is collapsing**,
enablement **cost scales linearly with reach**, and enablement **lags the technology it teaches**.
The result is a partner field that is perpetually trained for the product of six months ago, at a
cost that rises with every partner added.

## The idea: Constitutional Enablement

Rather than hard-coding courses, we define a small set of **durable principles** (a
"constitution") plus a governance mechanism for how those principles are instantiated, enforced,
and amended for each organization. The principles are **technology-agnostic** — written against
partner outcomes, not product features — so they survive product cycles and vendor change. The
instantiation is **AI-operated** — authored, personalized, delivered, and assessed by AI — so
reach and cost **decouple**.

## Why it scales asymmetrically

As an organization moves AI from authoring, to delivery, to assessment, to self-updating content,
the **marginal cost of enabling each additional partner trends toward zero while currency and
quality rise**. Traditional enablement scales linearly — more partners require more trainers,
content, and events. This framework breaks that link. That decoupling is the entire value
proposition.

## What it is, concretely

A **downloadable, white-labelable kit** an organization adopts and makes its own:

- A **constitution** — eight durable Articles and a maturity model (L0–L5).
- A **self-assessment** that locates the organization and routes it to a delivery path:
  **Trainer Cascade** (for organizations with L&D) or **Self-Serve Catalog** (for those without).
- **Competency maps** for sales and technical roles, written as demonstrable outcomes.
- An **engagement lifecycle** — Assess → Ratify → Instantiate → Operate → Enforce → Amend.
- A **customization mechanism** so each organization brands and tailors the kit, while a shared
  core keeps the standard consistent across the ecosystem.

## What we are asking {{ORG_NAME}} leadership to do

1. **Ratify** the eight Articles as the organization's enablement standard.
2. **Name the outcome signals** by which enablement will be judged — qualified pipeline, win
   rates on fit-for-purpose deals, successful implementations, retention — not completions.
3. **Authorize the climb** up the maturity model by applying AI to the enablement function, under
   the discipline of the Articles.

## What changes, and what it returns

| From | To |
| --- | --- |
| Content consumed | Capability demonstrated |
| Rebuilt each release | Continuously current |
| One curriculum for all | Adapted per role, maturity, vertical |
| Recall-based certification | Demonstrated competency |
| Cost rising with reach | Cost decoupled from reach |
| Measured by completion | Measured by outcomes |

The return is a partner field that is **current, demonstrably capable, and enabled at a cost that
no longer rises with the size of the ecosystem** — and a standard that travels intact across every
organization in that ecosystem.
