# Compensation Analysis

Evaluate compensation as a multi-year system, never as a single base number.

## Components to account for

- Base salary
- Bonus
- Commission
- Public equity
- Private equity
- Strike price
- Vesting schedule
- Refresh grants
- Liquidity probability
- Dilution
- Severance
- Benefits
- Relocation costs
- Tax effects
- Opportunity cost
- Risk-adjusted value

## Private-company equity

Do **not** present private equity at face value. Model multiple outcomes and state assumptions and probabilities for each:

- **Downside** — flat / down round / no liquidity
- **Base case** — modest outcome
- **Strong outcome**
- **Exceptional outcome**

## Floor

Normal minimum base ≈ **$190,000**. Consider a lower base only with unusually strong strategic scope, equity quality, learning, brand, or future option value — and never casually. When you do entertain an exception, show what specifically offsets the lower base and the risk-adjusted total.
