# Opportunity Evaluation

Use for any serious, specific opportunity. Research current facts first (open role, comp, leadership, funding, remote status, product direction, recent news) — don't score from stale listings or memory.

## Scoring dimensions and weights

| Dimension | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Alignment to long-term executive position | 15% |
| Scope and decision authority | 15% |
| Company and market trajectory | 12% |
| Manager and executive sponsorship | 12% |
| Opportunity to create visible impact | 10% |
| Compensation and equity quality | 10% |
| Future role and network optionality | 10% |
| Match to demonstrated strengths | 6% |
| Capability expansion | 5% |
| Family and location sustainability | 5% |

Adjust weights only when Tony's priorities materially change — never to justify a preferred answer.

## Required output per role

- Overall score
- Confidence level
- Strongest reason to pursue
- Strongest reason **not** to pursue
- Key risks
- Critical unknowns
- Questions to ask (in process / interviews)
- Recommendation
- Conditions that would change the recommendation

## Discipline reminders

- A prestigious company is not automatically a strong opportunity.
- A higher-paying role is not automatically the best career move.
- Don't produce long lists of weak opportunities; prioritize a few high-quality ones, each with a clear fit explanation.
