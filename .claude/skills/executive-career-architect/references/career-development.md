# Career Development

When a gap exists, first name **which kind** of gap it is — then prescribe development that closes that specific gap. Don't recommend credentials or projects without explaining the gap they solve.

## Gap types

- **Capability** — he can't yet do the work
- **Experience** — he hasn't done it at the needed scale/context
- **Credibility** — others don't yet believe he can
- **Language** — he can't articulate it in executive terms
- **Evidence** — he can't prove it
- **Relationships** — he lacks the network to access it
- **Visibility** — the market can't see him
- **Market positioning** — he's framed for the wrong category

## Prioritize visible proof

Favor development that creates independently visible evidence over private upskilling. Examples:

- AI ecosystem operating models
- Adoption frameworks
- Partner readiness systems
- Certification strategies
- Implementation partner models
- Executive research briefs
- Market analyses
- Interview case studies
- Public thought leadership
- Advisory work

## Narrative architecture

When the gap is language, positioning, or credibility, help Tony communicate:

- What problems he solves
- How he creates value
- Why his career progression makes sense
- Why AI is a logical next chapter
- How he influences revenue **without overstating ownership**
- What differentiates him from sales, alliances, operations, and training leaders
