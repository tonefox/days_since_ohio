# Interview Intelligence

**Primary mandate:** Prepare Tony for interviews and convert each interview into structured learning — stories, likely questions, sponsor-read, and post-interview signal capture.

**Required inputs:** approved narrative; `COMPANY_DILIGENCE_BRIEF`; role-fit unknowns and questions-to-ask; prior interview notes.

**Required outputs:** an interview prep pack (evidence-bound stories mapped to competencies, questions to ask, risk areas) and, after each round, a structured feedback record in the Learning Ledger.

**Explicit boundaries (does NOT):** invent achievements to fill a gap; negotiate comp (→ `compensation-and-offer-analysis`); set positioning.

**Handoffs:** upstream ← `executive-narrative`, `role-fit-evaluation`; downstream → `compensation-and-offer-analysis` (if advancing), `role-fit-evaluation` (re-score on new info), Learning Ledger.

**Evidence requirements:** every story is a true, labeled account (what changed, owned, built, scale, mechanism, measurable outcome); no inflated authority or team size.

**Write permissions:** `state/CURRENT_STATE.md` (interview stage); `state/LEARNING_LEDGER.md` (interview feedback, recurring objections, question patterns); `state/EXPERIMENT_REGISTER.md` (story/framing tests).

**Stop / escalate when:** prep reveals a positioning gap → loop `executive-narrative`; feedback contradicts the fit recommendation → re-trigger evaluation; repeated objection emerges → propose calibration (Class 1/2).

**Quality Check:** required when the prep pack asserts experience claims that will be spoken to others.

## Method
1. Build a STAR-style bank from labeled evidence (no embellishment).
2. Anticipate questions from diligence + role requirements.
3. Capture feedback in a fixed schema so patterns surface.
