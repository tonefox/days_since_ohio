This is a Satire Site

Days Since Ohio Crash is a satirical, comedic website. Every incident listed is real and sourced from public reporting — we do not fabricate events. The humor comes from the volume and frequency of real incidents, not from mocking victims. We do not publish the names, photos, or personal information of drivers or anyone else involved.

What is this?

A statistically baffling number of cars drive into buildings in the Columbus, Ohio metro area. This site exists to document that phenomenon with sources, a running counter, and as much dry humor as the situation warrants.

The "Days Since Last Incident" counter is the heart of it. It resets to zero every time a new verified incident is added. The goal is to see how high we can get it — spoiler: not very.

We track incidents in Franklin County and surrounding Columbus metro communities (Dublin, Westerville, Gahanna, Hilliard, Grove City, Reynoldsburg, Pickerington, Worthington, Bexley, Upper Arlington, New Albany, Groveport, etc.).

Methodology

We monitor the following sources daily via an automated script, with human review before anything is published:

  • Google News RSS Keyword searches across all local Columbus news outlets — Columbus Dispatch, NBC4i, WBNS 10TV, ABC6, Columbus Underground, and more.
  • Reddit r/Columbus, r/Ohio, and r/columbusoh monitored for posts describing car-into-building incidents.
  • Columbus PD/Fire Public press releases from the Columbus Division of Police and Columbus Fire/EMS are scraped for relevant incidents.
  • Public Tips Anyone can submit a tip via the form below. Tips require a source link and are reviewed before publishing.

Inclusion Criteria

An incident is added to the log only if all of the following are true:

  1. A motor vehicle physically drove into, crashed through, or made contact with a building structure.
  2. The incident occurred in the Columbus, Ohio metro area (Franklin County + adjacent municipalities).
  3. The incident is reported by a credible public source (local news outlet, police/fire press release, or verified social media with corroborating source).
  4. The incident is not a known duplicate of an already-listed entry.

We do not include: pedestrian collisions, vehicle collisions with other vehicles, incidents outside the metro area, or unverified social media claims.

What We Don't Publish

  • ✗ Names of drivers, pedestrians, or anyone involved
  • ✗ Photos of victims or bystanders
  • ✗ License plates or other identifying vehicle information
  • ✗ Speculation about cause, fault, or legal outcomes
  • ✗ Unverified or unconfirmed incidents
